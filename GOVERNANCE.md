# Governance

## Roles

- **Maintainers** — merge rights, release authority. Listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
- **Contributors** — anyone who submits a PR or issue.

## Decisions

Lazy consensus: a proposal (issue/PR) with no objection after 72 hours is accepted. Disputes are resolved by maintainer majority.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, quality contributions. Nominated by an existing maintainer, approved by majority.
